## 2. Workstreams & Technical Execution

### Workstream 1 — Pixel-Art Dungeon Environment Atlases (`Content/Art/Dungeon/*` & `ATLAS_Water_Surfaces_4x4`)
1. Generate rich 4x4 semi-pixelated dark fantasy sheets via AI `image-generator` subagent (or deterministic high-detail procedural pixel-art masonry fallback + Sobel normals) for:
   - `ATLAS_Dungeon_Floors_4x4` (dark indigo/slate flagstones, wet cobblestones & mossy drain tiles, brass compass inlays, cracked magma/spore/mirror tiles)
   - `PROP_Dungeon_Walls_And_Cutaways_4x4` (tall mossy stone blocks, low cutaway rubble stubs, 45° diagonal wedges, chain pillars)
   - `PROP_Doors_And_Level_Transitions_4x4` (Gothic carved stone archways, iron portcullis, descending stone stairs, 4-frame flickering iron sconces)
   - `PROP_Breakables_And_Traps_4x4` (wooden barrels/urns, floor grates/vents, hanging ceiling chains, bone chimes)
   - `PROP_Resting_Ground_Sanctuary_4x4` (campfire brazier, sensory eye monolith, chest/tent, glowing rune ring)
   - `ATLAS_Water_Surfaces_4x4` (dark navy/indigo stream channel with pixel-art current streaks & stone banks, transparent concentric ripple rings, mossy puddles, wet bootprints)
2. Process every sheet through `slice_normal_rig.py` (`--mode dungeon` / `--mode eco`) with border white-halo cleanup, Sobel tangent normals (`_N`), height/roughness/emissive maps (`_H`, `_R`, `_ORM`, `_E`), `AtlasMetadata.json`, and SHA-256 provenance in `Content/AssetManifest.json`.

### Workstream 2 — De-Haloing Flora, Crystals, Water & Fauna + Shader Balance
1. Upgrade `eco_flood_fill_chroma_key` in [`.agents/skills/sprite-sheet-25d-cutter/scripts/slice_normal_rig.py`](../.agents/skills/sprite-sheet-25d-cutter/scripts/slice_normal_rig.py):
   - Extend border-connected flood fill to trace through any border-connected dark background **and** adjacent white/light-grey sticker halo (`luma > 170` & `chroma < 48`), plus 1-px silhouette halo trim so crisp dark pixel outlines form the outer edge.
   - Re-process `PROP_Magical_Flora_And_Crystals_4x4`, `PROP_Water_And_Fauna_4x4`, and `PROP_Fauna_Critters_Strip_4x1`.
2. Tune `EmissiveCombiner` in [`DungeoneerEcoMaterialFactory.cpp`](../Source/DarkDungeoneer/Private/Rendering/DungeoneerEcoMaterialFactory.cpp) so crystal/flora hues preserve vibrant cyan, magenta, and emerald saturation instead of clamping to white, while boosting Tier 1 floor glow pool contribution (`1.35x` radial glow on stone).

### Workstream 3 — Reflective Stream Channel, Stone Banks & Entry Waterfall (`UDungeonWaterSubsystem`)
1. Replace `BuildProceduralWaterAtlas()` in [`DungeoneerAssetBootstrapSubsystem.cpp`](../Source/DarkDungeoneer/Private/Core/DungeoneerAssetBootstrapSubsystem.cpp) and load `ATLAS_Water_Surfaces_4x4_{D,N,E}` from `Content/Art/Ecosystem/` when present (plus upgraded deep-indigo/teal procedural generator when absent).
2. In [`DungeonWaterSubsystem.cpp`](../Source/DarkDungeoneer/Private/Ecosystem/DungeonWaterSubsystem.cpp):
   - Lower `HISM_Water` `EEcoCPD::Emissive` to `0.14f` (`Roughness = 0.03f`) so the water channel reads as dark reflective subterranean water catching the amber lantern and cyan crystal glow.
   - Anchor an upright camera-facing **Waterfall Cascade** (`PROP_Water_And_Fauna_4x4` Row 0) onto the Entry room's Upper-Left backdrop wall (`South`, `-Y`) pouring directly into the stream head, paired with churning splash ripples at the plunge pool.
   - Trigger visible concentric boot ripples around wading characters (`WalkStep` + periodic puddle wading ripples).

### Workstream 4 — Upright 2.5D Fauna/Sconces, Archways & Entry Diorama Composition
1. In [`DungeonFaunaSubsystem.cpp`](../Source/DarkDungeoneer/Private/Ecosystem/DungeonFaunaSubsystem.cpp), replace hardcoded `FRotator(55, 180, 0)` with `Bootstrap->GetCameraFacingSpritePlaneRotation()` and refine critter scale (`LumenMoth` `0.24`, `LanternBeetle`/`Newt`/`Crab` `0.30`), seeding 6 golden moths near the Entry lantern path and blooming flowers.
2. In [`DungeonFloraManagerComponent.cpp`](../Source/DarkDungeoneer/Private/Ecosystem/DungeonFloraManagerComponent.cpp), lay `PulseMoss` flat on the cobblestones (`FRotator::ZeroRotator` at `Z = 1.8 UU`), keep `PrismLily` / `SporeCap` / `EchoCrystal` upright camera-facing, cluster 3–4 magenta `PrismLily` blooms along both banks of the Entry stream with vibrant Tier 1 magenta/cyan/emerald floor pools, and clamp idle crystal emissive to `0.9f`.
3. In [`DungeoneerTileMapInstancer.cpp`](../Source/DarkDungeoneer/Private/Dungeon/DungeoneerTileMapInstancer.cpp):
   - Convert `SconceHISM` from `ConfigureBoxHISM` (`Cube.Cube`) to camera-facing cutout planes (`ConfigurePlaneHISM`) displaying `Doors_Sconce_D` / `Sanctuary_Brazier_D`.
   - Spawn carved Gothic stone archway portals (`Doors_Arch_D`) framing corridor exits on backdrop walls.
   - Allow foreground walls (`South`, `West`, `North`) to evaluate cutaway cleanly (`bCanCutawayForCamera = (Axis == South || Axis == North || Axis == West)`) while keeping `NorthWall` unaffected when only `SouthWall` is occluded.

### Workstream 5 — Isometric `-45°` Diorama Camera, Locked Darkness Exposure & Hero Washout Fix
1. In [`DungeoneerCharacter.h`](../Source/DarkDungeoneer/Public/Core/DungeoneerCharacter.h) and [`DungeoneerCharacter.cpp`](../Source/DarkDungeoneer/Private/Core/DungeoneerCharacter.cpp):
   - Set `CameraBoom` to `Pitch = -42.0f, Yaw = -45.0f`, `TargetArmLength = 1320.0f`, `TopDownCamera->FieldOfView = 35.0f` for classic 2.5D isometric diorama framing.
   - Lock exposure (`AutoExposureMinBrightness = 1.0f`, `AutoExposureMaxBrightness = 1.0f`, `AutoExposureBias = -0.35f`) so unlit dungeon corners drop cleanly into deep purple-black fog without grey washout.
   - Position `FlashlightComponent` at `+42 UU` ahead along the aim vector (`Z = 44 UU`) in `SyncFlashlightTransform()` so the cone starts **from the hand lantern** rather than blasting `HeroVisualMesh` from behind/center. Tune flashlight to warm `2800K` lantern gold (`FLinearColor(1.0f, 0.68f, 0.22f)`, `1850 lm`, `Inner = 15°`, `Outer = 30°`, `AttenuationRadius = 920 UU`) and lower `HeroLanternFillLight` to `95 lm` warm amber so hero pixel art stays dark-cloaked and crisp.
   - Transform screen-space WASD movement and mouse aiming by `CameraBoom` yaw (`-45°`) so `W` always moves visually Up-Screen and cursor aiming points accurately toward the mouse in isometric view.
2. In [`DungeoneerTypes.h`](../Source/DarkDungeoneer/Public/Core/DungeoneerTypes.h), [`DungeoneerAssetBootstrapSubsystem.cpp`](../Source/DarkDungeoneer/Private/Core/DungeoneerAssetBootstrapSubsystem.cpp), [`ZombieBase.cpp`](../Source/DarkDungeoneer/Private/AI/ZombieBase.cpp), and [`DungeonAtmosphereActor.cpp`](../Source/DarkDungeoneer/Private/Rendering/DungeonAtmosphereActor.cpp):
   - Synchronize `ComputeSpritePlaneOrientation()` with the active isometric camera view (`-42° Pitch, -45° Yaw`).
   - Add `OctantToScreenDirectionalRowForCameraYaw(Octant, CameraYawDeg)` for Hero & Zombie 4-row flipbooks while preserving `OctantToScreenDirectionalRow` verbatim for existing test compatibility.
   - Deepen `ADungeonAtmosphereActor` Volumetric Fog albedo toward violet-indigo obsidian (`(0.06, 0.04, 0.11)` for Crypt) and orient `AmbientMotesHISM` using `GetCameraFacingSpritePlaneRotation()`.
